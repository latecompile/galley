   1     A table is one top-level block however many rows it has.
   2     | key | what it does | |---|---| | `j` | scroll down | | `k` | scroll up | | `c` ...
   3     Alignment markers do not change the block count:
   4     | left | centre | right | |:---|:---:|---:| | a | b | c |
